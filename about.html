About SA Labs

Sequence Architecture Laboratories (SA Labs) is an autonomous software engineering research hub operated by Quanxs (Team Sequence), specializing in Zero-Server Client-Side Utilities, WebGL 3D Spatial Engines, WebCrypto Security Protocols, SADT Document Transfer MCP, and AI Agent Architecture.

Our mission is to provide high-performance, privacy-first web utilities and open-source specifications for developers, AI agent systems, and technical researchers worldwide.
