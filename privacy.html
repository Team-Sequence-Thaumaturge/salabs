Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Overview

Sequence Architecture Laboratories ("SA Labs", "we", "our", or "us") operates the portal at salabs.quanxs.com. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our website and tools.

2. Information Collection and Use

We prioritize zero-server, client-side data processing. Most tools on SA Labs process data locally within your web browser using WebCrypto, HTML5 Canvas, and WebAudio APIs without transmitting your input data to external servers.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our site. Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on user visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. Analytics

We utilize Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to collect anonymous usage metrics (such as page load times, country, and browser type) to improve user experience and portal performance.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our support page or email at support@quanxs.com.
